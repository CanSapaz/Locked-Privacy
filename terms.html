What the app does

Locked stores passwords, cards, notes and similar entries in an encrypted vault on your device, protected by a master password you choose. There is no account and no cloud service.

Your master password and your data

Only you know your master password. We cannot see it, reset it or recover it. If you forget it, or if your device is lost, damaged or reset without a backup, your vault cannot be recovered by us. You are responsible for keeping your master password safe and for keeping your own encrypted backups.
